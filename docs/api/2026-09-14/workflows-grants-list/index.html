GET List who has access to a workflow

/workflows/{workflow}/grants

Who can see, run or change a workflow: the people and groups given access to this workflow, and the access that covers every workflow in your organization. A person is shown by id, because their name is not part of the answer. Access given by someone else, or through a role, is listed too, with who gave it. You need view, edit or publish access to the workflow. A workflow you cannot see, or one that does not exist, is reported as not found.

Parameters

NameInTypeRequiredDescription
workflowpathstringyesThe workflow's id, from the list of workflows.

Response 200

FieldTypeRequiredDescription
grantslist of objectyesAccess given on this workflow alone. A grant can be one of several.
inheritedlist of objectyesAccess that covers every workflow in your organization and so reaches this one. It cannot be taken away from here.
